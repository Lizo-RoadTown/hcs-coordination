## What Tapestry is (hero)

**Eyebrow:** The instrument

**Headline:** See your living map. Work it with your agents.

**Lede:**
Tapestry is where your living map becomes something you can see and use. Your systems and the
people connected to them show up in one place, kept current, and you and your AI agents work on
them side by side.

**Button:** See it in your organization → (to HCS, or to contact)

## What you get

One place where your whole operation is laid out and stays current, and where the help you get
improves the more you use it. You see where you are strong, where you are at risk, and what to do
next.

## How people use it

You and your team open Tapestry and see your systems and people in one view. You work with AI
agents that remember your context, so you are not starting over each time. The more you work in
it, the better you and your agents coordinate, and the more of your repeated work becomes
reusable.
